  1. F ¯ = ( A + B ) ⋅ C ⋅ D ¯ + E \overline{F} = \overline{\left( A + B \right) \cdot C \cdot D} + E

  2. F ¯ = ( A + B ) ⋅ C ⋅ D ⋅ E ¯ ¯ \overline{F} = \overline{\left( A + B \right) \cdot C \cdot D \cdot \overline{E}}

  3. F ¯ = A ¯ ⋅ B ¯ ¯ ⋅ C ⋅ D ⋅ E ¯ ¯ ¯ \overline{F} = \overline{\overline{\overline{\overline{A} \cdot \overline{B}} \cdot C \cdot D \cdot \overline{E}}}

La solución correcta es la 'c'.
