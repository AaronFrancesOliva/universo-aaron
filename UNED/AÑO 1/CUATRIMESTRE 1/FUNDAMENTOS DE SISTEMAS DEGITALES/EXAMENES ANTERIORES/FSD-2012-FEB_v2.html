De los 4 pares de expresiones dados a continuación ¿Cuál es el que corresponde a las expresiones duales del Teorema de Adyacencia?

  1. A + A ¯ = 1 ,   A ⋅ A ¯ = 0 A + \overline{A} = 1,\quad A \cdot \overline{A} = 0

  2. A ⋅ B + A ⋅ B ¯ = A ,   ( A + B ) ⋅ ( A + B ¯ ) = A A \cdot B + A \cdot \overline{B} = A,\quad\left( A + B \right) \cdot \left( A + \overline{B} \right) = A

  3. A ⋅ B + A = B ,   ( A + B ) ⋅ A = B A \cdot B + A = B,\quad\left( A + B \right) \cdot A = B

  4. A ⋅ B + A = A ,   ( A + B ) ⋅ A = A A \cdot B + A = A,\quad\left( A + B \right) \cdot A = A

La solución correcta es la 'b'.
